# 🛒 Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
SQL analysis of a US retail superstore dataset containing 9,994 orders
across 3 product categories and 4 regions, covering 2014–2017.

## Business Questions & Key Findings

### 🌍 Regional Performance
- West is the top region  $725k in sales and 14.94% profit margin
- Central region underperforms  only 7.92% margin despite $501k in sales

### 📦 Category Insights
- Technology is the most profitable category  $145k total profit
- Furniture is barely profitable  only $18k profit due to heavy discounting

### 💸 Discount Problem
- Furniture receives the highest average discount at 17%
- High discounts are directly killing Furniture profit margins

### 🗺️ Loss Making States
- 10 states are losing money  Texas is worst at -$25,729
- Ohio and Pennsylvania follow at -$16k and -$15k respectively

### ⭐ Star Products
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier is the most profitable product at $25,199
- All top 10 profitable products are Technology items

### 📈 Sales Trend
- Sales grew 52% from 2014 ($484k) to 2017 ($733k)
- Consistent growth except a small dip in 2015

## Advanced Analysis

### 🏆 Customer Segmentation
- Only 20 VIP customers drive majority of revenue  averaging $13,245 each
- 88 Loyal customers spend 3.5x more than one time customers
- 510 returning customers show healthy repeat purchase behaviour

### 🗺️ State Performance Rating
- 38 states rated Excellent  business is largely healthy
- District of Columbia leads with 36.98% profit margin
- 10 states are Loss Making  Ohio worst at -21.69% margin

### 📦 Product Segmentation
- Furniture loss making products have highest avg sales at $2,615
- The more Furniture sold, the more money is lost
- Office Supplies has 729 high margin products — most of any category
